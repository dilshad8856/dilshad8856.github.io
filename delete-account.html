Delete your NutriTime account and data

NutriTime, by Muhammad Dilshad

In the app (fastest)

Delete your account

  1. Open NutriTime and go to Profile.
  2. Tap Account.
  3. Tap Delete account, then confirm.

Your account is deleted straight away.

Delete everything on your phone

  1. Go to Profile, then Privacy & data.
  2. Tap Delete all my data, type DELETE and confirm.

This removes your plan, records, reports, name, photo, settings and reminders from the phone. Uninstalling the app also removes them.

Without the app

If you no longer have the app or your phone, email nutritime11@gmail.com from the Gmail address you signed in with, with the subject "Delete my NutriTime account". We will delete your account within 30 days and email you when it is done. We may ask you to confirm it is your account.

What is deleted, and what is kept

DataWhat happens
Account (Google account ID, email address, name, profile picture link)Deleted. Firebase removes it from its backups within its stated period, normally up to 180 days.
Plan, records, reports, name, photo, settingsThese were only ever on your phone. Delete them in the app (above) or uninstall the app. We never had a copy, so we cannot delete them for you.
Export files you savedThese are yours, wherever you saved them. Delete them yourself.
Premium purchase recordsKept by Google Play as its own records, for tax and law. Deleting your account does not cancel your subscription: cancel it in Google Play.
