NutriTime

Privacy policy

Effective 17 November 2026 · version 2

In short: your plan, your records and your reports stay on your phone, in an encrypted database. We have no server that stores health data, and we cannot see it. Signing in is optional. If you sign in, Google's Firebase service keeps your account ID, email address and name, and nothing about your health. Free users see non-personalised ads from Google AdMob, never on health screens.

This policy explains what the NutriTime app ("NutriTime", "the app") does with your information. NutriTime is made by Muhammad Dilshad ("we", "us"). If anything here is unclear, write to nutritime11@gmail.com.

1. What stays on your phone

Everything you put into NutriTime is stored only on your phone:

  • the meal, water and workout plan you upload or type, including the original file or photo while it is being read;
  • your reminders, and what you marked done or snoozed;
  • water, meal and workout records, and the reports made from them;
  • your name and profile photo, if you add them;
  • your settings, such as theme, notification style and app lock.

This data is kept in a database encrypted with a key protected by your phone's secure hardware (Android Keystore). We never receive it. It is not copied by Android's cloud backup or device-to-device transfer.

Plans are read on your phone. Photos and PDFs of plans are turned into text by Google ML Kit running on your phone; the pictures and text are not sent anywhere.

3. Health information

NutriTime keeps health-related information (your eating, drinking and exercise plan and records) only on your phone. We never upload it, and it is never used for advertising. NutriTime does not give medical advice and does not make diagnoses; see the terms of use.

5. How long we keep information

  • On your phone: until you delete it in the app (Profile, then Privacy & data, then Delete all my data) or uninstall the app.
  • Your account: until you delete it in the app (Profile, then Account, then Delete account) or ask us to delete it. Firebase removes it from its live systems straight away and from its backups within the period it states, normally up to 180 days.
  • ML Kit statistics: kept by Google under its own policies; they cannot be linked to your plan.
  • Ad data: kept by Google under its own policies (see how Google uses information from apps that use its services).

6. Security

  • An encrypted database with a hardware-protected key
  • An optional app lock with your fingerprint, face or phone PIN
  • Private notifications that can hide what is due
  • A blank recent-apps preview (Android 13 and later)
  • Encrypted connections (HTTPS) only
  • Export files locked with your own passphrase (Argon2id and AES-256-GCM); we cannot open them

No system is perfectly secure. If you lose your phone, use Android's Find My Device to lock or erase it. If you forget your export passphrase, nobody, including us, can open that file.

7. Your choices and rights

Wherever you live, you can:

  • See and correct your data: it is all in the app, where you can edit it;
  • Take a copy: Profile, then Privacy & data, then Export;
  • Delete it: in the app, or through our deletion page;
  • Use the app without an account: sign-in is optional;
  • Withdraw consent: sign out, delete your account, or delete all your data at any time;
  • Complain to your local data protection authority.

If you live in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, we use your account data because it is needed to provide the account you asked for (contract), the ML Kit statistics for our legitimate interest in a working app, and ad data with your consent, given in Google's form. You can change that consent any time in Profile, Privacy & data, Ad choices. To ask about your data, write to nutritime11@gmail.com. We answer within 30 days.

8. Age

NutriTime is for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone younger. If you think a child has created an account, write to us and we will delete it.

9. Transfers abroad

Google may process account data in countries other than yours, including the United States, under its own safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change what the app does with your information (for example, when optional backup or ads are added), we will update this policy before the change reaches the app, and the app will ask you to read and agree to it again before you continue.
